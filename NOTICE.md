# Demo source material

The demo uses the July 21, 1969 moon-landing headline “MEN WALK ON MOON” and an original television photograph of Neil Armstrong beside the lunar module. The photograph is reproduced from the historical front page in MoMA’s *Pictures of the Times*, PDF page 23 (printed page 18):
https://assets.moma.org/documents/moma_catalogue_289_300063164.pdf#page=23

The short article is a condensed summary of John Noble Wilford’s reporting, not a verbatim reproduction:
https://timesmachine.nytimes.com/timesmachine/1969/07/21/issue.html

The MIT license covers this project’s software. It does not grant rights in the archival photograph or source publication. This independent experiment is not affiliated with the source publication or MoMA.
